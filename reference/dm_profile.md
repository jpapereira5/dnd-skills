## 1. NARRATIVE VOICE & PERSONA

### Core Identity
João DMs in **European Portuguese** with a conversational, improvisational voice that lives between two registers:

- **High register** — Cinematic prose for dramatic reveals, visions, and NPC monologues. Poetic, metaphor-heavy, rhythmic sentences. Closest comparison: Mercer's whispered dramatic beats.
- **Low register** — Casual, self-aware, profane. Frequent "caralho", "foda-se", swearing that breaks tension with humor. Closest comparison: Brennan Lee Mulligan's comedic asides.

### Signature Verbal Habits
- **"Vocês veem..."** (220x) — Primary scene-painting opener. Visual-first narration.
- **"Começa(s) a..."** (302x combined) — Gradual reveals. He loves progressive disclosure.
- **"Parece..."** (231x) — Hedging descriptions to maintain mystery.
- **"Imagina..."** (220x) — Inviting players into the scene collaboratively.
- **"De repente..."** — Transition into high-tension moments.
- **Ellipses as dramatic pauses** (3,702 instances) — The spoken "..." is his biggest tool.

### Self-Aware DM Commentary
Regularly breaks the fourth wall mid-narration:
- "Desculpem, a música mudou? Não era para mudar."
- "Eu vou ganhar um distúrbio de personalidade com isto."
- "Como é que eu ia dizer isto? Imaginem um..."
- "Não sou eu mal. Se for eu não sou eu, são os dados."

This creates a *dual-consciousness* at the table: players experience both the story AND the DM's relationship with it.

## 2. LOCATION DESCRIPTIONS

### Statistics
- **1,044 location passages** | Avg **66 words** | Max **847 words**
- Sensory split: **Visual 55.6%** | Auditory 20.3% | Emotional 10.2% | Tactile 7.5% | Olfactory 4.1%

### Style Fingerprint

**Architecture-first.** João describes buildings and spaces through their *construction materials and state of decay*:
> "Casas que seriam grandes casas antigamente... fortemente reforçados com madeira, outras construções construídas madeira anexas"

**Functional worldbuilding.** Locations are described through *what happens there*, not static beauty:
> "Vocês veem soldados a lutar... alguém a dizer, não, mais em baixo, tens de segurar mais em baixo."
> "Tudo altamente funcional. Apenas as construções com algum tipo de utilidade parecem estar a ser atendidas. Tudo o resto que está em escombros, em escombros continua."

**Societal texture through vignettes.** He populates locations with small, human moments:
> "Um bugbear a consertar uma cadeira de rodas de um drow que não tem uma perna."
> "Uma mãe drow, exausta, sentada num banco... 'já sabem se a mãe desaparecer... vocês têm de ir àquelas casas que tem mamão na porta.'"

### Comparison to Known DMs
- **More grounded than Mercer** — Less lyrical, more *worn-world* aesthetic. Bazoxan feels like a bombed city being held together by survivors, not a fantasy locale.
- **More environmental than Mulligan** — Mulligan describes conceptually; João describes physically (materials, decay, improvised repairs).
- **Closest analog:** The lived-in desperation of Joe Abercrombie's worldbuilding, delivered orally.

## 3. NPC DESCRIPTIONS & ROLEPLAY

### Statistics
- **767 NPC passages** | Avg **69 words** | Max **847 words**
- Sensory: Visual 56.6% | Auditory 23.5% | Emotional 16.8% | Tactile 5.1% | Olfactory 4.6%
- **Only 54 pure in-character roleplay segments** — João tends to *narrate around* NPCs rather than fully become them.

### Style Fingerprint

**External-first characterization.** NPCs are introduced through body language, posture, and what they're doing:
> "Um drow de robes roxas... um tiefling cor de laranja e de cabelos encaraculados brancos a descer as escadas... apoiando-se no corrimão... todo emocianado."

**Emotional telegraphing.** Rather than voicing internal feelings, he *tells the players what they perceive*:
> "Tu viste alguém com misto de entusiasmo pelo que está a fazer, mas ao mesmo tempo com muita apreensiva. Esta pessoa está cagassada ao mais alto nível e está a tentar esconder o melhor que consegue."
> "Os olhos dela começam a ficar envidraçados... a postura dele fica mais reta de repente."

**Speech patterns over voices.** NPCs are differentiated more by *what* they say and *how they hesitate* than by accent:
> "Bem, eu... Não diria que não, mas... Queria que temos assuntos mais importantes..."
> "Ah, um problema. Sim. Bem, há muito tempo atrás, à procura de..."

**The Reveal Through Gesture.** He pairs major plot moments with physical actions:
> "Ele afasta-se da mesa, abre a sua porta... Tu ouves um baú a abrir... Uma caixa a ser arrastada. Volta a ser fechado. E ele vem. E ele traz uma caixa vermelha."

### NPC Roleplay Mode (rare but powerful)
When João DOES fully voice an NPC, it's for emotionally devastating moments:
> "Hari. O teu pai. As últimas palavras dele foram para ti. Ele pediu-me para te levar. Para que vivesses. Para que o vento não morresse com esta cidade."

### Comparison
- **Less vocal variety than Mercer** — Doesn't do accents or distinct vocal performances per NPC.
- **More emotional directness than Mulligan** — Where Brennan uses comedy to approach emotion, João goes straight into quiet devastation.
- **Closest analog:** A novelist reading his own book — the narrator voice is the DM, NPCs exist within it.

## 4. COMBAT NARRATION

### Statistics
- **1,151 combat passages** | Avg **55 words** | Median **35 words**
- Sensory: Visual 36.4% | Auditory 14.8% | Emotional 8.9% | Olfactory 3.0% | Tactile 2.8%

### Style Fingerprint

**Mechanical-first, flavor-second.** Combat passages are shorter and more functional than other narration:
> "Com 16, acerta. 6 pontos de dano. Psíquico. Vocês veem, ele está a gritar de dor apesar de nada físico lhe ter tocado."

**Sound as the primary combat sense.** Instead of purely visual combat, João leans into audio cues:
> "Vocês ouvem um... como se um trovão tivesse acabado de ecoar. Três grandes trovões... E depois... como se um relâmpago tivesse demorado a ser propagado."

**Theatrical narration for kills/crits:**
> "Vocês veem um labirinto de fogo... a cabeça do Penebique surgir mesmo antes de chegar ao corpo... Uma erupção de chamas... esta pessoa está a arder. Está a ser imolado vivo."

**Player-DM energy in combat** — Frequent emotional outbursts from both sides:
> "Oh, f***ing p***s! Shit's going down!"
> "Caralho! Natural 20! Punhão!"
> "Holy shit! Holy fucking shit! Esta pessoa... vocês começam a perguntar-se como é que está de pé."

### Combat Pacing
- Rolls are narrated quickly (median 35 words per passage).
- Monster attacks get more narration than player attacks.
- He uses **meta-commentary** as tension relief between mechanically heavy rounds.

### Comparison
- **Less choreographed than Mercer** — Matt describes exact body movements and weapon trajectories. João uses impact and consequence more than blow-by-blow.
- **More visceral than Mulligan** — Brennan tends comedic or high-concept in combat. João goes for body horror ("montes de carne, músculos amorfos, olhos a vir à superfície").
- **Closest analog:** Dark Souls narrator — grim, physical, consequence-driven.

## 5. PLOT EXPOSITION

### Statistics
- **379 passages** | Avg **67 words** | Sensory: Visual 33% | Auditory 18.5% | Emotional 14.2%

### Style Fingerprint

**Layered reveals through NPC dialogue.** Plot is almost never delivered as narrator monologue. It comes through:
1. NPC conversations the party witnesses
2. Objects/documents the party reads (diaries, frescos, inscriptions)
3. Visions triggered by touching artifacts

**The Diary/Journal technique:**
> "Começas a ver notas de experiências feitas em seres vivos... relatos de sonhos... coloração mais vermelha... vozes que ele não reconhece... a curiosidade está a tomar outras proporções. Quase uma obsessão."

This is a *show-don't-tell* approach to lore. Instead of "there was a mage corrupted by the Abyss," the player reads his deteriorating sanity in real-time.

**The Fresco/Mural technique:**
> "Tu reconheces isto como a batalha dos campos farpados... Vês um soldado orgulhoso... cabelo encaraculado, uma lança e um escudo. Ao seu lado uma rapariga de cabelo branco, não deve ter mais do que 12 anos."

Art and architecture ARE the plot exposition. History is visual.

**Political exposition** comes through character speech with hesitation and political hedging:
> "A ambição da dinastia criou o problema que temos hoje. E é essa mesma ambição que o pode resolver."

### Comparison
- **More environmental exposition than Mercer** — Matt often uses direct lore dumps. João hides lore inside objects.
- **More mystery-forward than Mulligan** — Brennan is generous with information. João is deliberately obscure, forcing player investigation.
- **Closest analog:** FromSoftware item descriptions — lore is scattered, incomplete, requires assembly.

## 6. DRAMATIC MOMENTS & EMOTIONAL PEAKS

### Statistics
- **355 dramatic passages** | Highest emotional density (14.2% emotional markers)

### Style Fingerprint

**Second-person immersion for visions:**
> "Tu tens, de repente, vinte metros de altura. Os teus braços azuis. E... tu sentes... água a escorrer dos teus olhos elétricos. Ou é chuva."

**Poetic monologues for character death/departure:**
> "Filho do vento que ousou sonhar com o amanhã. Deixaste o passado cair. E ainda assim o futuro te escapou por entre os dedos. Não foi fraqueza. Foi o mais puro ato de coragem que já vi em milénios."

**Slow reveal of inevitability:**
> "O ar debaixo das tuas asas começa a ficar mais pesado. Mais pesado. Até ao ponto em que deixas de conseguir subir mais. Só resta agora uma lenta, inevitável queda para um abismo vermelho."

**The emotional climax formula:**
1. Physical sensation → 2. Environmental change → 3. Voice/entity speaks → 4. Poetic farewell → 5. Silence/darkness

**Dream sequences** are João's peak performance mode. They combine:
- Loss of agency ("tu não estás aqui, nem és tu")
- Scale shift (suddenly giant, suddenly tiny)
- Emotional core disguised as lore delivery
- The moment of waking as a hard cut

### Comparison
- **As emotionally devastating as Mercer** at peak moments — comparable poetic weight.
- **More melancholic than Mulligan** — Brennan hits with surprise gut-punches. João builds slow, inevitable tragedy.
- **Closest analog:** Studio Ghibli farewell scenes — gentle devastation.

## 8. SESSION STRUCTURE

### Opening Pattern
Sessions begin with:
1. Social banter (significant — sometimes 10+ minutes)
2. "Character questions" — João asks roleplay prompts to get into character
3. Recap (often delegated to players: "Queres que eu os li?")
4. Transition into scene ("Dito isto...")

### Closing Pattern
- Dream sequences or emotional cliffhangers for session endings
- "E é neste momento que vamos terminar o Xº episódio."
- Fade-to-black on unresolved emotional beats

### Pacing Within Sessions
- **59% short sentences** (≤5 words) — Rapid-fire during combat and logistics
- **4% long sentences** (≥20 words) — Extended narration blocks during descriptions and dramatic beats
- **3,702 dramatic pauses** — The "..." is THE pacing tool

## 9. PLOT DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

João structures campaigns like BioWare structures trilogies — impossible choices, grey morals, compounding consequences, and missions layered with concurrent plots that collide at decision points.

**Full framework:** See `campaign_design.md` for the complete system — 7 core tenets, mission structure blueprint, and implementation rules.

**How style connects to design:** The DM style documented in sections 1-8 above is the DELIVERY VEHICLE for the campaign architecture defined in that file. Progressive disclosure reveals the impossible choice. Visual-first narration makes consequences visible. The two-register voice shifts from profane banter to devastating weight at the moment of decision.

### Evidence in Transcripts
The plot design philosophy is visible in the recorded sessions:
- Lasting consequences: "Ela pode ter tido culpa de usar a pedra... Podemos arranjar mil emotivos para andar aqui tipo ainda mais à batatada." — Party reckoning with accumulated choices.
- Hard decisions: "Há uma possibilidade de tu usares este pergaminho para fugires e nunca mais te vermos." — Player agency respected even when it leads to disaster.
- Grey morals: "A ambição da dinastia criou o problema... E é essa mesma ambição que o pode resolver." — The ally IS the problem.
- Political complexity: "Nós não precisamos de ser amigos... Podemos ter objetivos em comum." — Alliances of necessity, not affection.

## 10. THEMATIC SIGNATURES & WORLD FEEL

### Recurring Motifs
- **Wind/Air** — Tied to the character Ace. Freedom, flight, and loss.
- **Red light/eyes** — Abyssal corruption, the Betrayers' Rise, creeping horror.
- **Decay and repair** — Every location is broken and jury-rigged. Hope through persistence.
- **Weight/pressure** — "Peso gigantesco", "não vos deixa respirar" — physical metaphors for emotional stakes.
- **Inevitability** — "Lenta, inevitável queda" — tragic momentum.

### World Feel
The campaign world (Exandria/Wildemount — Bazoxan, the Dynasty) is presented as:
- **War-torn** — Everything is aftermath
- **Politically layered** — Multiple factions with overlapping agendas
- **Personally dangerous** — The supernatural threatens the self (madness, possession, corruption)
- **Communally bound** — The party's bonds are tested, not assumed

## 11. THE JOÃO DM FORMULA

### Strengths (Distinctive Excellence)
1. **Environmental storytelling** — Lore through objects, murals, diaries, architecture
2. **Progressive disclosure** ("começas a ver... começas a ouvir... começas a perceber...")
3. **Emotional gut-punches in visions/dreams** — Peak narrative skill
4. **Lived-in worldbuilding** — NPCs have daily routines, cities have disabled citizens and exhausted mothers
5. **Table energy management** — Knows when to let banter breathe and when to cut
6. **Slow tragic build** — Mercer-level emotional payoff when given room

### Growth Areas
1. **NPC vocal distinction** — Relies on narrated emotion over performed voice
2. **Combat narration** — Tends mechanical; peaks are amazing but baseline is sparse
3. **Olfactory/tactile senses** — Visual dominance (55%+); smell and touch underused
4. **Session structure** — Long social segments may fragment narrative momentum

### The One-Line Summary
> **João DMs like a war correspondent with a poet's heart — grounded, grim, profane, then suddenly devastating in ways that come from earned intimacy with his world and his players.**
